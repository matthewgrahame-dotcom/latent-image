THE FULL ARCHIVE — TAP ANY PLATE
Point your camera at a scene and Latent Image surfaces historic photographs with a similar composition — light, structure, symmetry, where the eye lands — along with what makes each one work and how to recreate it with the camera you already have. Matching happens entirely on your device, comparing composition and tonal qualities rather than recognising objects or scenes, so a match reflects shape and light, not subject matter. Most photographs shown are the real historic image, drawn from public archives; a small number of especially well-known photographs are still under copyright, and for those you'll see the same analysis and technique guide without reproducing the photograph itself. Nothing is uploaded. Live viewfinder frames are analysed in memory and discarded; when you capture an attempt, a compressed copy and its analysis are saved only on this device so you can revisit your progress.
